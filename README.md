### plot
The Plot of the movie follows the story of J. Robert Oppenheimer, One of the most influential minds of the modern world and was reponsible to the creation and testing of the first Atomic Bombs during the 
Manhattan Project at the heights of the Second World War. The movie is loosely based on the book called "American Prometheus" which tells the story of oppenheimer at full accuracy. The Story is formatted in a non-linear way in which tells tyhe different parts of life of Oppenheimer. The movie explores the absolute progress of man when it comes to the motivation of war. It also entails the phrase of "Man playing God" as Oppenheimer's creation led to the most destructive weapon humanity has ever seen. After the Hiroshima and Nagasaki Bombings, Oppenheimer beleived that his creation, he in and of itself is responsible for the deaths of the innocent and in the future will be responsible for it altogether if nuclear war ever comes to fruition.
